# Hook patterns (X / Twitter)

> Purpose: stop the scroll in ~1 second. Pick **one** pattern; do not mix.

## 1) Contrarian take
- Template: “Most people believe X. That’s why they get Y. The better move is Z.”
- Notes: keep X common, Y painful, Z specific.

## 2) Specific result (outcome-first)
- Template: “I did X for N days and got Y. Here’s exactly what worked.”
- Notes: numbers beat adjectives.

## 3) Counterintuitive truth
- Template: “The thing that feels wrong is what works: Z.”
- Notes: follow immediately with a mechanism or example.

## 4) Mistake + fix
- Template: “Stop doing X. Do Y instead. Here’s why.”
- Notes: the “why” must be a concrete cause, not vibes.

## 5) Open loop
- Template: “I used to think X. Then I learned Y. The turning point was Z.”
- Notes: pay off within 2 tweets (or 2 lines).

## Hook checklist
- [ ] One clear promise (what will I get?)
- [ ] One concrete proof (number / detail / example)
- [ ] No hedging (“maybe”, “kinda”, “sometimes”)
