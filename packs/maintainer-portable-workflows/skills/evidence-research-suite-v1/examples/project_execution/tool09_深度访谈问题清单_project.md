# 工具9：深度访谈问题清单 — product AI-Native 家庭日历机

> **方法论**：20类专业访谈问题设计  
> **背景**：product正在开发AI-Native家庭日历机/家庭AI中心。已完成1051条Amazon评论JTBD分析，验证4个核心Job。现需通过深度访谈进一步验证Prepare阶段隐形工作、家庭会议场景、AI主动服务接受度。  
> **受访用户**：已使用家庭日历类产品（Skylight/Google Calendar/白板等）6个月以上的北美家庭日程管理者，25-55岁，有至少一个学龄儿童  
> **语言**：英文

---

## 访谈三大验证目标

1. **Prepare阶段的隐形工作有多真实/多痛苦？**
2. **用户对「AI主动干预日程」的接受阈值在哪？**
3. **「家庭会议主持人」是真场景还是理想化？**

---

## 1. 背景问题（Background）— 激活自我叙述

> 目的：帮助受访者快速进入信任和自我披露状态

**1a.** Tell me about a typical weekday morning in your household. Walk me through from when you wake up to when everyone is out the door.

**1b.** How would you describe your role in managing your family's schedule? If you had to give yourself a "job title" for this role, what would it be?

**1c.** Who in your family is the "keeper of the schedule"? How did that happen — was it a choice or did it just evolve?

**1d.** Can you describe a moment recently when you felt really proud of how smoothly your family's day went? What made it work?

---

## 2. 情境问题（Contextual）— 重构生活场景

> 目的：通过具体场景还原真实行为和动机

**2a.** Think about the last time a schedule change caught you off guard — what happened? Walk me through how you handled it.

**2b.** Can you describe the last time you had to prepare something for your child's school event? What was the process from when you found out to when everything was ready?

**2c.** When you sit down on Sunday evening to think about the week ahead, what does that look like? Where are you? What tools do you use?

---

## 3. 知识性问题（Knowledge-Based）— 探索认知框架

> 目的：了解用户如何理解和解释自己的行为

**3a.** What does "being organized" mean to you when it comes to family life? How would you know you've achieved it?

**3b.** If you had to explain to a friend what "mental load" is, how would you describe it in your own words?

**3c.** What do you think is the difference between a family that "runs smoothly" and one that "is always in chaos"? What makes the difference?

---

## 4. 历史问题（Historical）— 揭示塑造当下的过往

> 目的：理解过去经历如何塑造当前行为

**4a.** How did your parents manage the family schedule when you were growing up? How has that influenced how you do it now?

**4b.** Was there a specific moment or event that made you think "I need a better system for this"? What happened?

**4c.** What family management tools or methods have you tried and abandoned? What made you stop using them?

---

## 5. 态度问题（Attitude）— 揭露价值体系

> 目的：通过表面态度挖掘深层价值观

**5a.** Some people say "a good parent never forgets anything." How does that statement make you feel?

**5b.** How do you feel about the idea that AI could help manage your family's schedule? Is that exciting, concerning, or something else?

**5c.** If someone told you "your family doesn't need technology to be organized," what would you think?

---

## 6. 认知问题（Cognitive）— 挖掘认知图式

> 目的：理解用户解释自己行为的底层逻辑

**6a.** Why do you think you're the one who ends up managing most of the family schedule? Is it because you're better at it, or is there another reason?

**6b.** When something falls through the cracks — a missed appointment, a forgotten item — how do you explain why it happened?

**6c.** Do you think being organized is a skill, a personality trait, or a resource issue? Why?

---

## 7. 期望问题（Expectation）— 投射理想自我

> 目的：探索用户对未来版本的想象

**7a.** If you had a perfect "family assistant" that could do anything — what's the single most important thing you'd want it to handle for you?

**7b.** Imagine it's next year and your family scheduling is running perfectly. What does that look like? How is it different from now?

**7c.** If this device could give you back one hour every day, what would you do with that time?

---

## 8. 竞争问题（Competitive）— 暴露心理参照

> 目的：通过比较揭示用户心理竞争对象

**8a.** Do you ever look at how other families manage their schedules? Have you seen something that made you think "I wish I could do that"?

**8b.** Compared to your closest friend's family, how would you rate your family's organization level? What makes you feel that way?

**8c.** If you could "steal" one organizational habit from any family you know, what would it be?

---

## 9. 社交问题（Social）— 揭示透过他人眼光的自我定义

> 目的：理解用户如何通过他人目光定义自己

**9a.** When friends or family come to visit, do you feel the need to have things "put together"? What does "put together" look like for your home?

**9b.** Has anyone ever commented on how organized (or disorganized) your family is? How did that make you feel?

**9c.** If a guest saw a smart family calendar on your wall, what impression do you think it would give them about your family?

---

## 10. 频率问题（Frequency）— 识别行为节律

> 目的：通过回忆关键使用时刻识别行为模式

**10a.** In a typical week, how many times do you check or update your family calendar? Is there a pattern — certain times of day or days of the week?

**10b.** Think about the last time you had that "oh no, I forgot something" moment related to family scheduling. How often does that happen?

**10c.** How often does your family sit down together to talk about the upcoming schedule? Is it a regular thing or does it happen randomly?

---

## 11. 学习能力问题（Learnability）— 揭示对新工具的心理反应

> 目的：评估自我效能感、认知极限和学习动机

**11a.** When you first started using [their current calendar tool], what was the hardest part to get used to? How long before it felt second nature?

**11b.** If a new device required a 30-minute setup to connect all your family's calendars, would that feel reasonable, too long, or you'd need someone to help?

**11c.** How do you typically learn to use new technology — do you read instructions, watch videos, just start tapping, or ask someone?

---

## 12. 障碍问题（Barrier）— 暴露心理防御结构

> 目的：通过挫败反应揭示防御机制

**12a.** What is the single biggest frustration you've experienced with your current family scheduling method?

**12b.** Have you ever almost switched to a new calendar tool but decided not to? What stopped you?

**12c.** If a device promised to solve all your scheduling headaches but cost $500, what would make you hesitate? What would make you go for it?

---

## 13. 兼容性问题（Compatibility）— 评估心理对齐度

> 目的：评估用户与产品在功能/情感/身份层面的适配

**13a.** How important is it to you that a family scheduling tool works with BOTH Google Calendar and Apple Calendar without any issues?

**13b.** A product like this would be mounted on a wall in your home — where would you put it? Does it fit with how your home looks and feels?

**13c.** Do you see a family calendar device as more of a "practical tool" or a "lifestyle statement"? Why?

---

## 14. 恢复问题（Recovery）— 揭示失败后的内心修复

> 目的：了解系统崩溃/体验中断时用户如何恢复内心平衡

**14a.** When your current calendar system fails — sync breaks, reminders don't fire — what do you do? How do you recover?

**14b.** If you had been relying on a smart device for scheduling and it stopped working, what would your "backup plan" be?

**14c.** Think of a time when everything fell apart schedule-wise — a week where nothing went right. How did you get things back on track?

---

## 15. 优先级排序问题（Prioritization）— 通过取舍暴露价值序列

> 目的：通过强制排序揭示决策原则

**15a.** If you could only have ONE of these, which would you choose and why?  
   - (a) A device that shows everyone's schedule at a glance  
   - (b) A device that automatically prepares checklists for tomorrow  
   - (c) A device that detects schedule conflicts and suggests fixes

**15b.** Rank these from most important to least: (1) Never miss an event (2) Reduce time spent on scheduling (3) Get family members to contribute more (4) Feel less stressed about family logistics

**15c.** You have $500 to spend on improving family life. Would you put it toward a smart calendar device, a house cleanering service for 2 months, or a family vacation fund? Why?

---

## 16. 冗余问题（Redundancy）— 探索心理安全感需求

> 目的：理解用户为何需要"额外功能"来维持心理安全

**16a.** Even if you had a perfect digital calendar, would you still want a physical backup — like a paper calendar on the fridge? Why or why not?

**16b.** Do you ever add the same event in multiple places "just to be safe"? What drives that behavior?

**16c.** If the device were very reliable, would you feel comfortable getting rid of ALL your other scheduling methods — whiteboard, phone calendar, paper lists?

---

## 17. 关系问题（Relational）— 揭示与产品的情感依附

> 目的：理解用户如何与产品形成情感连接

**17a.** With your current calendar tool, is there a feature that you'd genuinely miss if it disappeared? What is it and why does it matter?

**17b.** Has your current scheduling system ever made you feel genuinely grateful? Can you describe that moment?

**17c.** If this device had a "personality" — like it could greet your family in the morning with the day's schedule — would that be charming or creepy?

---

## 18. 隐含问题（Implicit）— 通过投射揭示无意识动机

> 目的：使用隐喻和投射表面化无意识需求

**18a.** If your family's scheduling system were a person, who would it be? Why that person?

**18b.** If you could describe the feeling of a well-organized family day in one image or metaphor, what would it be?

**18c.** Imagine this device has been in your home for a year and has become essential. Now someone tries to take it away. What's the first emotion you'd feel? What would you fight to keep?

---

## 19. 假设问题（Hypothetical）— 暴露隐藏决策逻辑

> 目的：通过想象情境揭示潜在需求和边界

**19a.** If this device could automatically generate a "what to prepare tonight for tomorrow" list, but it sometimes gets things wrong — would you rather it tries and occasionally errors, or not try at all?

**19b.** If the device needed to listen to family conversations to learn your patterns — like picking up "don't forget the water bottle" and adding it to a list — where is the line between helpful and invasive for you?

**19c.** If you had to choose between a device that is 100% private but can't learn your patterns, or one that deeply learns your family but stores data in the cloud — which would you lean toward?

**19d.** Imagine the device could "take over" weekly meal planning based on your family's preferences and dietary needs. Would you welcome that or does it feel like it's taking too much control?

---

## 20. 比较问题（Comparative）— 揭示心理参照系

> 目的：通过对比揭示用户的价值判断框架

**20a.** How is managing a family's schedule similar to or different from managing a team at work? Which is harder for you?

**20b.** Compared to 5 years ago, has managing your family's schedule gotten easier or harder? What changed?

**20c.** If you compare the "whiteboard on the fridge" approach to "a smart AI calendar device" — beyond the technology, what fundamentally changes about how your family operates?

**20d.** Think about the best tool/app/system you've ever used for anything. What made it great? How would a family calendar device need to be similar?

---

## 访谈流程建议

| 阶段 | 时间 | 问题类型 | 目的 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 暖场 | 0-5min | 1 背景 | 建立信任 |
| 日常重构 | 5-15min | 2 情境 + 10 频率 + 4 历史 | 还原真实场景 |
| 深层挖掘 | 15-30min | 3 知识 + 5 态度 + 6 认知 | 暴露价值体系 |
| 需求探索 | 30-45min | 7 期望 + 19 假设 + 15 优先级 | 挖掘潜在需求 |
| 产品对话 | 45-55min | 11 学习 + 12 障碍 + 13 兼容 | 评估产品接受度 |
| 收尾升华 | 55-60min | 18 隐含 + 17 关系 | 表面化深层动机 |
